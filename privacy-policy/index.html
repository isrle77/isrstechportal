Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

This policy explains what information ISRS Tech Portal (isrstechportal.com, "the site") collects and how it is used.

Images and files you check

Our tools process files locally in your web browser. When you select an image in the Print Size & DPI Checker, the browser reads its pixel dimensions on your device. The image is not uploaded, transmitted, or stored on our servers, and we cannot see it. Closing or refreshing the page discards it.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, the hosting provider that serves the site may record standard technical information such as IP address, browser type, the page requested, and the time of the request. These logs are used for security and to keep the site running.

Cookies, analytics, and advertising

The site does not currently set its own tracking cookies. In the future we may add:

  • Analytics (for example Google Analytics) to understand which pages and tools are used, in aggregate.
  • Advertising (for example Google AdSense) to keep the tools free. Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info.
  • Affiliate links, which may use cookies to credit us when you buy from a partner.

If these are added, this policy will be updated, and where required by law we will ask for your consent before setting non-essential cookies. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

Email

If you email us, we use your address and message only to reply. We do not sell or share it.

External links

The site links to other websites, such as Etsy. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

Children

The site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have rights to access or delete personal data we hold about you. Contact us to make a request.
