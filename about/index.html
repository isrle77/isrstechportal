About ISRS Tech Portal

Small, focused tools for people who make and sell things online.

ISRS Tech Portal is a growing collection of free web tools for creators, designers, and Etsy sellers. The idea is simple: many everyday questions in an online shop have a precise answer, but finding it usually means digging through forum threads or doing math on a phone calculator. Each tool here answers one of those questions quickly and accurately.

What you will find here

The first tool is the Etsy Print Size & DPI Checker, which tells you how large an image can be printed and how sharp it will be at every common frame size. An Etsy fee and profit calculator is in development, and more tools will follow.

How the tools are built

  • Private: tools run entirely in your browser. Files you check stay on your device.
  • Lightweight: plain, fast pages that work well on phones.
  • Transparent: each tool explains the math it uses, so you can check the results yourself.

Who is behind it

The site is run by the creator of DesignsToGetInspired, an Etsy shop for printable designs and digital templates. The tools started as utilities for that shop and are now shared for anyone to use.
